Relink Pages

A page is autolinked when an ingest touches it, against the titles that existed at that moment — so a page written before its subjects had pages keeps those mentions as plain text. This re-runs the autolinker over every page and picks all of them up.

It is slow on purpose: the work per page grows with the number of pages, so a large wiki takes tens of minutes. It runs in the background — you can leave this page and come back, and normal use is unaffected. Changed pages get a history entry, so anything it gets wrong is revertable.
